Training: find the faultThree results this toolkit once got wrong, on its own worked examples. Each
is shown as the report gave it. Find what is wrong with it; the finding and what was done about it
are shown once you have.Training, three exercises in finding what a report got wrong, on the worked
examples, is in the browser app only, under Start. It needs no network and changes no
project.Progress0 of 3 faults foundKept in this browser, not in the project: it is yours, and goes
with no project file.1. The 30-minute testDr. Timbo's Residence, pumping testWhat the report saidA
constant discharge test at 2.93 m³/h, in a borehole cased at 5 inches.The pump ran for 30 minutes.
When it stopped the water level had fallen 32.8 m and was still falling.The report adopted a
transmissivity of 1.4 m²/day and recommended a safe yield of 0.97 m³/h.What should have stopped that
yield being recommended?AThe pumping rate was too low to stress the aquifer.BThe test ended while
the pump was still emptying the casing, so the readings describe the borehole and not the
aquifer.CThe static water level was not measured before pumping.DA constant discharge test cannot
give a transmissivity; only a step test can.2. The aquifer 80 m deepRokel, geophysicsWhat the report
saidTwo Schlumberger soundings, each laid out to an AB/2 of 80 m.Both models end in a layer of 47 to
50 ohm-m that starts about 8 m down, with nothing found beneath it.The report said: water bearing
zone from 8 m to 80 m, aquifer thickness 72 m, drill to 80 m.Where did the 80 m come from?AFrom the
bottom of the water bearing layer, which the model placed at 80 m.BFrom the electrodes: 80 m is the
largest AB/2 the crew laid out, not a depth at which anything was found.CFrom the depth of a
borehole nearby, used as a guide.DFrom the resistivity: ground of 47 to 50 ohm-m is water bearing to
80 m.3. The site 250 km awaySite details, any projectWhat the report saidA position was read off a
phone and typed into the site details as 8.4657, 13.2317.The app took it. The site was drawn inside
Sierra Leone, about 250 km from the village, and every map and report used that position.What was
wrong with what was typed?AThe latitude and the longitude were typed the wrong way round.BThe
longitude has lost its minus sign: Sierra Leone is west of Greenwich, so its longitudes are
negative.CThe position was in degrees and minutes, not decimal degrees.DThe phone's GPS was too
inaccurate to use.
