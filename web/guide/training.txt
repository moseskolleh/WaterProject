Settings About & method Training: find the fault Three results this toolkit once got wrong, on its
own worked examples. Each is shown as the report gave it. Find what is wrong with it; the finding
and what was done about it are shown once you have. Training, three exercises in finding what a
report got wrong, on the worked examples, is in the browser app only, under Start. It needs no
network. Answering changes no project; opening a worked example from it replaces the project that is
open. Progress 0 of 3 faults found Kept in this browser, not in the project: it is yours, and goes
with no project file. 1. The 30-minute test Dr. Timbo's Residence, pumping test What the report said
A constant discharge test at 2.93 m³/h, in a borehole cased at 5 inches. The pump ran for 30
minutes. When it stopped the water level had fallen 32.8 m and was still falling. The report adopted
a transmissivity of 1.4 m²/day and recommended a safe yield of 0.97 m³/h. What should have stopped
that yield being recommended? A The pumping rate was too low to stress the aquifer. B The test ended
while the pump was still emptying the casing, so the readings describe the borehole and not the
aquifer. C The static water level was not measured before pumping. D A constant discharge test
cannot give a transmissivity; only a step test can. 2. The aquifer 80 m deep Rokel, geophysics What
the report said Two Schlumberger soundings, each laid out to an AB/2 of 80 m. Both models end in a
layer of 47 to 50 ohm-m that starts about 8 m down, with nothing found beneath it. The report said:
water bearing zone from 8 m to 80 m, aquifer thickness 72 m, drill to 80 m. Where did the 80 m come
from? A From the bottom of the water bearing layer, which the model placed at 80 m. B From the
electrodes: 80 m is the largest AB/2 the crew laid out, not a depth at which anything was found. C
From the depth of a borehole nearby, used as a guide. D From the resistivity: ground of 47 to 50
ohm-m is water bearing to 80 m. 3. The site 270 km away Site details, any project What the report
said A position was read off a phone and typed into the site details as 8.4657, 13.2317. The
Streamlit app took it. The site was drawn inside Sierra Leone, about 270 km from the village, and
every map and report used that position. What was wrong with what was typed? A The latitude and the
longitude were typed the wrong way round. B The longitude has lost its minus sign: Sierra Leone is
west of Greenwich, so its longitudes are negative. C The position was in degrees and minutes, not
decimal degrees. D The phone's GPS was too inaccurate to use.
