page did; leave empty otherwise Before pumping Schafer's rule puts the end of casing storage at 0.6
(dc² − dp²) divided by the specific capacity. Before any drawdown is read, the specific capacity
comes from the transmissivity range above through Logan's T ≈ 1.22 Q/s. Over that range, storage
lasts 30.7 min to 5 h 07 min. The low end sets the time. Once the level is being read, the drawdown
gives a period of its own; that one only grows while the level is falling, so it takes over from the
cautious figure when the readings have passed it. If the pump starts now, do not stop before 15:37.
The shortest test that can give a transmissivity here is 5 h 07 min, set by the casing-storage
period. The test as planned, 4 h 00 min, is shorter than that. Plan to pump until 15:37 at least.
