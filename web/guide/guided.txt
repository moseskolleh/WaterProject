of 8 steps complete 1 Record the site Client, community, chiefdom, district and GPS. Every report
header and every map comes from this. Go 2 Site the borehole Upload the VES sheet. The app inverts
each sounding, interprets the layers and ranks the drilling targets. Go 3 Design the borehole
Screens against the aquifer, casing, gravel pack and sanitary seal, drawn to scale. Go 4 Test the
borehole Cooper-Jacob, Theis and recovery, then a safe yield and a pump setting depth. Go 5 Check
the water WHO and national limits, ionic balance, corrosivity and the Piper and Stiff diagrams. Go 6
Cost the works A bill of quantities on the RWSN model, with cost and price kept apart. Go 7
Supervise construction Stage checklists and the numeric acceptance checks a supervisor applies on
site. Go 8 Hand over The handover report, the data sheet and the operation and maintenance guidance
