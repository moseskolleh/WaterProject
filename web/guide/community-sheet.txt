Project summary, works completed, the borehole data sheet, water quality, operation and maintenance
guidance, the committee and the signature block. Not ready to certify - outstanding: Field data,
Site position, Yield established. Error Field data — Readings that were never measured are in this
project: dr_timbo_water_quality.xlsx. The values in that file were invented to demonstrate the
toolkit, so no result derived from them describes anything that was sampled. Error Site position —
No GPS position is recorded on any sheet in this project. A borehole that cannot be found again on
the ground cannot be certified, revisited or maintained. Error Yield established — The safe yield of
0.39 m3/h (range over the assumptions, 0.22 to 0.71) is indicative, not established: the test pumped
for 30 minutes, below the 240 needed to see late-time behaviour, so the yield is extrapolated 4.2
log cycles of time; the whole test lies inside the 117-minute casing-storage period, so its drawdown
is the borehole emptying rather than the aquifer responding; no transmissivity method fitted to
standard and the Cooper-Jacob value is adopted as the best available. The report will still be
produced, stamped PROVISIONAL and listing these items. To issue it as an interim document instead,
record who is issuing it and why. Issued by Reason Record override Assumptions carried into this
report: dr_timbo_drilling_log.xlsx: Intervals, times, penetration rates, water strikes, grouting
depth and dates are transcribed from the WiNGiN completion report. The lithology descriptions are
illustrative reconstructions guided by the borehole diagram annotations - the original log leaves

is safe to drink, who to call, how to report a fault and what to keep away from it. It says a little
less than the pumping test allows, never more, and says so where something is not known. Hours the
pump is used each day for the amount of water in a day Borehole identifier as on its plate, from the
Asset registry What the sheet will say This sheet was made from example data. It is not about a real
borehole and must not be handed out. About 10 jerrycans of water in an hour. No, not as it is. Treat
this water before you drink it. What it could not say, and why A bundled example is loaded, so the
sheet says across its top that it is not about a real borehole. Enter the hours the pump is used
each day to print a daily amount. Without it the sheet gives the amount in an hour. The yield is
indicative, so the sheet calls the amount a first estimate. No committee member is entered, so the
sheet leaves lines to write on. No borehole identifier is given, so the sheet names no plate. Type
the identifier the Asset registry gave the borehole. The sheet is in English. A Krio version waits
for a translation checked by native speakers; none is machine made. Community sheet (.docx) Evidence
