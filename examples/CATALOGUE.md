# Worked examples

Generated by `python examples/build_catalogue.py`. Every count below
is read back out of the workbook by the reader the toolkit uses, and
every verdict is read off the report's own cover.

3 case(s). `examples/README.md` says which parts of each
dataset are transcribed from a real document and which are illustrative;
read it before quoting a number out of a generated report.

## Rokel - geophysical survey

`python examples/run_rokel_geophysics.py` -> `examples/projects/rokel/`

Parse, consistency checks, inversion against the original IPI2Win models, hydrogeological interpretation and a drilling preference order.

- Site: Rokel, Western Area district (Living Water International)
- Inputs:
  - `rokel_ves.xlsx` (ves): 2 sounding(s), 36 reading(s)
  - `rokel_ipi2win_models.xlsx` (ipi2win): 2 original model(s), 7 layer(s)
- Reports:
  - `Rokel_Geophysical_Survey_Report.docx`: certifiable
- Figures: 12; derived tables: 2

## Kuntoloh - step drawdown test

`python examples/run_kuntolo_step_test.py` -> `examples/projects/kuntolo/`

The pending-yield path: curves and available drawdown now, transmissivity and yield once somebody supplies the discharges the sheet never recorded.

- Site: Kuntoloh, Port Loko district (ACF)
- Inputs:
  - `kuntolo_step_test.xlsx` (pumping): step+recovery test, 92 water level reading(s), 3 step(s), no discharge on the sheet, 4 flag(s) raised by the reader
- Reports:
  - `Kuntolo_Pumping_Test_Report.docx`: provisional - outstanding: Pumping test measured, Site position, Yield established
- Figures: 6

## Dr Timbo - drilling to handover

`python examples/run_dr_timbo_completion.py` -> `examples/projects/dr_timbo/`

Drilling log to borehole design and drawing, constant discharge test, water quality assessment, and the completion, quality and handover reports.

- Site: Dr. Timbo's Residence, Western Area Rural district (Dr. Timbo)
- Inputs:
  - `dr_timbo_drilling_log.xlsx` (drilling): 14 lithological interval(s) to 70 m
  - `dr_timbo_constant_test.xlsx` (pumping): constant+recovery test, 11 water level reading(s), 1 step(s)
  - `dr_timbo_water_quality.xlsx` (quality): 28 determinand(s)
- Reports:
  - `Dr_Timbo_Borehole_Completion_Report.docx`: provisional - outstanding: Field data, Site position, Yield established
  - `Dr_Timbo_Handover_Report.docx`: provisional - outstanding: Field data, Site position, Yield established
  - `Dr_Timbo_Water_Quality_Report.docx`: provisional - outstanding: Field data, Site position
- Figures: 9

## Packs

`python examples/build_catalogue.py` writes one zip per case into
`examples/packs/`, holding the input workbooks, the reports, the
figures and any derived tables. They are built rather than committed:
every file in them is already in the repository, and they are
byte-reproducible, so a second copy would only go stale.
